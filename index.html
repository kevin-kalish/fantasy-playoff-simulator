Fantasy Playoff Simulator
In Development
Monte Carlo Fantasy Football Analytics

Know your path to the playoffs.

Fantasy Playoff Simulator is an analytics application under development that estimates playoff and seed probabilities by combining league standings, remaining schedules, player rosters, projections, NFL matchups, and simulated player performance.

Built for uncertainty.

A single projection cannot capture the range of outcomes in fantasy football. The simulator is being designed to run thousands of possible seasons and quantify how roster strength, weekly matchups, player volatility, and the remaining schedule affect each team's postseason outlook.

Playoff Probability

Estimate each team's chance of qualifying for the fantasy playoffs across thousands of simulated seasons.

Seed Probability

Model the probability of every possible finishing position rather than relying on a single projected final standing.

Weekly Matchups

Incorporate player projections, NFL opponents, roster construction, byes, and performance variation into weekly outcomes.

What-If Analysis

Evaluate how wins, losses, lineup decisions, roster changes, and potential trades could alter postseason probability.

Yahoo Fantasy Integration

Read-only league analytics

Fantasy Playoff Simulator is being developed to allow Yahoo Fantasy Football users to authorize read-only access to their fantasy leagues. League information will be used solely for fantasy football analysis and simulation. The application will not make roster moves, transactions, lineup changes, or league modifications.

Development status

The application is currently in private development and testing. Initial development focuses on a 10-team Yahoo Fantasy Football league, with broader league support planned as the simulation and data-integration layers mature.
